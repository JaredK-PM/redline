# Explore law firm referral/affiliate partnership

Reach out to [law firm contact] via email to inquire about:
- Referral program structure (do they refer clients to contract review tools?)
- Affiliate partnership terms (would they refer ReviewIt to their SMB/freelancer client base?)
- Commission/revenue share model

## Rationale

Law firm referral networks are a strong go-to-market channel for the ReviewIt target segment (freelancers, small business owners, gig workers who sign contracts without legal counsel). A referral partnership could:
- Establish credibility (endorsed by counsel)
- Reach warm leads (referred by trusted advisor)
- Create recurring revenue (affiliate/referral fees)

## Success criteria

- Contact made and conversation initiated
- Response received with program details or willingness to explore
- Next steps documented (if any)

## Notes

This is a discovery task, not a commitment. Goal is to understand if this channel is viable and what terms/effort it would require.
